Master's thesis at the University of St. Gallen, supervised by [Dr. Justus Vogel](https://www.unisg.ch/en/university/about-us/organisation/detail/person-id/9acbc1c5-f703-41b5-8fd5-cfb5d2edc825/) and co-supervised by [Prof. Dr. Jana Mareckova](https://sites.google.com/view/janamareckova/). Expected submission: 17 November 2026.

A simulation study with known ground truth that evaluates whether causal forest methods and their variable importance measures correctly identify the variables driving heterogeneous treatment effects in clinical data. A fuller description will follow once the thesis is submitted.
